# Thermal prediction (0.2.4)

The forecast uses a lumped first-order RC model: heat loss H in W/K and effective capacity C in kWh/K. It starts from the complete set of indoor thermometer readings and uses the validated learned pair when enabled, otherwise manual H and configured C. The exact constant-input solution handles partial first hours. Configure optional preheat margin (0–3 °C) under Energy and forecast; default is disabled. Baseline maintains the target, alternative raises it during hours with positive predicted solar generation. This is a scenario, not an optimized solar dispatch: gross heating costs do not subtract PV, household consumption or battery benefits, and solar/internal heat gains and room differences are not modeled. No actuator calls. Insufficient generator capacity or missing prices are marked as unavailable heat rather than silently assuming delivery. Live HA validation remains necessary.
