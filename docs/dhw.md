# Domestic hot water (optional)

Configure ACS/DHW in integration options. Select the water heater or switch, optionally map temperature, power and cumulative energy sensors, and enter verified tank capacity and rated input power. Reference temperatures are advisory values, not appliance setpoints. The integration estimates ideal sensible heat from water volume and measured temperature; losses, stratification and draw-off are not modeled. Solar scheduling ranks contiguous future hours by potential overlap with gross PV production, not available surplus. Suggestions require a known temperature at or above the configured minimum and sufficient forecast coverage. No device commands are issued; all manufacturer sanitation and safety cycles remain unchanged. Daily consumption learning and joint battery/space-heating optimization are not implemented. Runtime testing on Home Assistant remains necessary.
