# Energy and forecast

Select existing Home Assistant PV power, positive-export grid power, battery percentage and hourly weather entities. All optional. Current energy readings are displayed, not treated as free future electricity. The hourly planner requires a configured heat loss and target temperature and technically configured generators. It estimates steady-state heat demand for the next 24 available hourly forecast observations, applies configured COP curves, and compares feasible single-source costs at the current electricity tariff. No extrapolation of future solar production, storage state or tariff is made. No commands are sent. This is a first advisory forecast, not a full thermal storage or battery optimization. Runtime validation in Home Assistant remains required.
